What to Eat for Dinner: 12 Dinner Ideas That End the Debate

Dinner is the hardest meal to decide. The day is done, everyone is hungry, and nobody wants to be the one who picks — so the question goes round in circles. These 12 dinner ideas are all real dishes from the What to Eat Wheel, so every one of them comes with its ingredients, a time and a budget level. Pick one, or let the wheel pick for you.

How to decide in under a minute

  1. Check the clock. Under 30 minutes? Stick to the quick plates above — noodles, pasta and burrito bowls. Got an hour? Lasagna-style comfort food and roasts open up.
  2. Check who is eating. If anyone at the table is vegetarian, filter to the veg dishes first — the green dot marks them.
  3. Check the budget. $ Cheap dishes keep the week affordable; save the $$$ Treat plates for the weekend.
  4. Stop at one spin. If you still cannot choose from the list, spin the wheel once and cook whatever it lands on — a decision made beats a perfect decision still being debated.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to eat for dinner when I can't decide?

Pick a dish from the list above, or tap the Dinner Dilemma preset on the wheel and spin. It filters the wheel to dinner dishes only, so one spin gives you one dinner idea with its ingredients.

What is a quick dinner idea for a weeknight?

Veg Hakka Noodles takes 20 minutes, and Penne Arrabbiata and Mac & Cheese take 25 minutes. All three are on the wheel with their full ingredient lists.

What are good vegetarian dinner ideas?

Margherita Pizza, Veg Hakka Noodles, Mac & Cheese, Penne Arrabbiata and Veg Burrito Bowl are all vegetarian dinner dishes on the wheel, marked with a green veg dot.

What should I eat for dinner on a budget?

Look for the $ Cheap dishes: Veg Hakka Noodles, Penne Arrabbiata, Meatloaf & Mashed Potatoes and Vegetable Soup are all budget-friendly dinner options on the wheel.
