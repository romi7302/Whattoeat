# What to Eat Wheel

A free tool that helps you decide what to eat. Spin the wheel, filter by breakfast, lunch or dinner, veg or non-veg, time and budget, and get a dish with its ingredients instantly.

Live site: https://whattoeatwheel.netlify.app

## Files

- `index.html` — the wheel
- `about.html` — about this tool
- `suggest.html` — suggest a dish
- `sitemap.html` — site map
- `privacy-policy.html` — privacy policy
- `terms-and-conditions.html` — terms and conditions
