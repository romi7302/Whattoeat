What to Eat for Lunch: 12 Lunch Ideas for Every Workday

Lunch has to do two jobs at once: fill you up and fit into a short break. These 12 lunch ideas are all real dishes from the What to Eat Wheel — sandwiches, salads, bowls and burgers — and most take 25 minutes or less. Pick one for today, or spin the wheel and let lunch decide itself.

How to decide in under a minute

  1. Count your minutes. Ten to fifteen minutes means a sandwich, a wrap or a salad. Twenty-five minutes opens up burgers, bowls and pasta.
  2. Light or filling? Salads and buddha bowls keep the afternoon light; burgers, cheesesteaks and club sandwiches are the filling option.
  3. Veg or non-veg? Half of the list above is vegetarian — look for the green dot — so a veg lunch is never the boring option.
  4. Make it or order it? Every dish here works both ways: cook it at home with the ingredient list, or order the same thing and skip the cooking entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to eat for lunch when I have no time?

Tuna Salad Sandwich takes 10 minutes, and Club Sandwich, Greek Salad and Cheese Pizza Slice take 15 minutes. All are on the wheel with their ingredients.

What is a healthy lunch idea?

Quinoa Buddha Bowl and Greek Salad are the lighter lunch options on the wheel, and Caesar Salad and Cobb Salad are filling salads with protein. The wheel also has a Healthy Lunch preset.

What are good vegetarian lunch ideas?

Veg Fried Rice, Pasta Alfredo, Falafel Wrap, Quinoa Buddha Bowl, Greek Salad and Tomato Soup & Grilled Cheese are all vegetarian lunches on the wheel.

What should I eat for lunch at work?

Pick something that travels well and eats fast: a club sandwich, a falafel wrap or a tuna salad sandwich. If you are ordering in, a chicken burger or a Philly cheesesteak works too.
