About Us

What to Eat Wheel is a free tool that answers one small question with a big daily cost: what should I eat today? Set a few filters, spin the wheel, and you get one dish — with the ingredients you need to make it.

No account, no download, no sign-up. It runs entirely in your browser.

How it works

  1. Set your filters — meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack), veg or non-veg, cooking time and budget. Or tap a preset like Dinner Dilemma, Breakfast in 15 Min or American Classics to skip the thinking entirely.
  2. Spin the wheel — click the SPIN button or tap the white circle in the middle of the wheel.
  3. Cook or order the result — the result card and popup show the dish, its tags and the full ingredient list. Tap Spin Again if you want a second opinion, or Copy Result to send it to someone.

What's on the wheel

The wheel holds 100 dishes, each with a cooking time, a budget level and an ingredient list:

  • 49 American — BBQ ribs, Philly cheesesteak, meatloaf, shrimp & grits, jambalaya, New York-style pizza, biscuits & gravy and more
  • 11 Italian, 9 Continental, 8 Healthy, 8 Chinese, 6 Mexican
  • 4 Middle Eastern, 2 Japanese, 2 British, 1 Mediterranean

You can also add your own foods in the Inputs panel — family favorites and fridge leftovers work best. Your additions stay in your browser session only.

Who is it for

Anyone who has ever stood in front of an open fridge and said "I don't know what to eat" — busy weeknights, families deciding together, meal preppers, and anyone who would rather spin than scroll through recipes.
