What Should I Eat? Light Meals and Snacks When You Can't Decide

Sometimes you are not hungry for a full meal — you just want something. A snack, a light bite, something small that ends the "what should I eat?" loop without a big cooking session. These 12 light meals and snacks are all real dishes from the What to Eat Wheel, and most take 25 minutes or less.

How to decide in under a minute

  1. Ask how hungry you are. Properly hungry? Chicken wings, nuggets or nachos. Just peckish? Hummus and pita, popcorn or roasted chickpeas.
  2. Ask how much time you have. Ten minutes means hummus and pita, banana bread or popcorn. Fifteen to twenty covers fries, nuggets and mozzarella sticks.
  3. Alone or sharing? Nachos, popcorn, fries and cheese balls are easy to share; a pita with hummus is a solo snack.
  4. Still stuck? Spin the wheel once with the snack filter in mind and eat whatever it lands on — a small decision should not take longer than the snack itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I eat when I'm not very hungry?

Go small: Hummus & Pita takes 10 minutes, Popcorn Bowl takes 10 minutes, and Roasted Chickpeas take 25 minutes. All three are light snacks on the wheel.

What is a quick snack I can make in 10 minutes?

Hummus & Pita, Banana Bread Slice and Popcorn Bowl all take 10 minutes, and Nachos & Cheese and Mozzarella Sticks take 15 minutes.

What should I eat for a light meal?

Hummus & Pita with a salad on the side, or Roasted Chickpeas, or Garlic Bread with soup — all light options from the wheel's snack and lunch lists.

How do I stop asking what should I eat?

Set one rule and stick to it: pick the first dish that fits your time and diet, or spin the wheel once and eat the result. The wheel exists so the decision takes five seconds, not five minutes.
