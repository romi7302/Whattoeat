      1. What is What to Eat Wheel?

      A free spin wheel built for American kitchens. Instead of scrolling through lists or asking “what should we eat?” five times, you set your meal, veg or non-veg, time and budget — spin — and get one clear answer in about 5 seconds. From BBQ ribs and Philly cheesesteaks to meatloaf, mac & cheese and fresh salads, it covers the comfort food Americans actually crave.

      Every result is something you can actually eat today: a real dish from 100 built-in ideas, with its main ingredients shown right away, so you can cook it or order it without a second decision.

      2. How to Use This Spinner?

      The wheel is easy to use. Set your filters, spin, and cook or order the result. The steps below work the same on phone and laptop.

      2.1 Set Your Filters

      Pick a preset like American Classics or Dinner Dilemma to skip the thinking entirely — or set the four filters yourself: meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), veg or non-veg, time (up to 15, 30, or 30+ minutes) and budget ($ cheap, $$ medium, $$$ treat). Ten matching dishes load onto the wheel, and the Inputs panel on the right shows exactly what is on it.

      2.2 Spin the Wheel & Get Your Result

      Click the SPIN button or tap the white circle in the middle of the wheel. The wheel spins, slows down, and lands on one dish. The result appears in the card next to the wheel and in a popup — the dish, its veg/non-veg tag, cuisine, time and budget, plus the ingredients you need. Tap Spin Again if you want a second opinion, or Copy Result to send it to someone.

      2.3 Add Your Own Foods

      Use Add your own foods under the Inputs panel. Your family favorites, leftover ideas, anything — they join the wheel instantly for this session. In Text Mode you can also paste a whole list, one dish per line, and load it onto the wheel at once. In the Inputs list, the eye button hides a dish from the wheel and the × button removes it from this wheel.

      3. Wheel Customization

      The wheel adapts to your mood, not the other way round:

      • Presets — Surprise Me, Dinner Dilemma, Breakfast in 15 Min, Healthy Lunch, American Classics, Fast-Food Night and Budget Bites.
      • Filters — meal, diet, time and budget, combined however you like.
      • Inputs panel — see the 10 dishes on the wheel, hide or remove any of them, and add your own.
      • New 10 Options — reshuffles a fresh set of 10 matching dishes onto the wheel.

      4. Save & Share Your Wheel

      There is no sign-up and nothing to save to an account. Your filters and custom foods live in this browser session only — close the page and they are gone. To share a decision, use Share in the top bar or Copy Result on the result card: it copies the dish name with its ingredients, ready to paste into any chat.

      5. Browse All Food Ideas

      Every dish on the wheel, in one place. Search it, or tap any dish to see its ingredients in the result card above.

      6. What to Eat for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner & Snacks

      Not sure which meal you are even deciding? Start from the meal — each one loads the wheel with dishes for that time of day. Looking for breakfast ideas, lunch ideas or dinner ideas? Pick a meal below and spin for instant meal ideas matched to your diet, time and budget — or read our 12 lunch ideas, 12 dinner ideas, 10 ideas for what to cook tonight and quick ideas for what to eat first. For a balanced plate, the USDA's MyPlate guide shows what a healthy meal looks like.

      7. Frequently Asked Questions

      What should I eat today?

      Spin the wheel above. It picks randomly from 100 real dishes matched to your meal, diet, time and budget — so instead of a generic list, you get one decision you can act on right now, with ingredients included.

      What to eat for dinner when I can't decide?

      Tap the “Dinner Dilemma” preset and spin. It filters the wheel to dinner dishes only. Still stuck after one spin? Spin again — or remove the thinking completely and cook whatever the second spin says.

      Is there a veg-only option?

      Yes. Set the Veg / Non-Veg filter to Veg and every dish on the wheel is vegetarian. There's also an American Classics preset with comfort-food favorites like meatloaf, mac & cheese and BBQ ribs.

      Can I add my own foods to the wheel?

      Yes — use “Add your own foods” in the Inputs panel. Your items join the spin instantly for this session. Family favorites and fridge-leftover ideas work best. You can also suggest a dish for the wheel.

      Is the What to Eat Wheel free?

      Completely free, no sign-up, no download. It runs entirely in your browser as a single lightweight page — which is also why it loads fast on any phone or laptop.

      What should I cook tonight?

      Tap the “Dinner Dilemma” preset and spin — the wheel gives you one dinner idea with its ingredients, so you know exactly what to cook tonight. One spin, one decision, no more “I don't know”.
