How to decide in under a minute

  1. Open the fridge first. If you already have pasta, rice or tortillas, start there — the dish that uses what you have is the fastest dinner tonight.
  2. Pick a time limit. Twenty minutes or less means noodles, stir-fry or arrabbiata. Thirty minutes opens up pizza, fajitas and ramen.
  3. Match the mood. Comfort food night? Mac & cheese or Alfredo. Something lighter? Burrito bowl or stir-fry.
  4. One spin settles it. If two dishes both sound good, spin the wheel once with the Dinner Dilemma preset and cook the winner — no re-spins.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I cook tonight if I only have 20 minutes?

Veg Hakka Noodles and Veg Stir-Fry & Noodles both take 20 minutes. Penne Arrabbiata, Mac & Cheese and Veg Burrito Bowl take 25 minutes.

What is the easiest thing to cook tonight?

Start with what is already in your kitchen. If you have pasta, Penne Arrabbiata is one of the simplest dinners on the wheel — penne, tomato, garlic, chilli and basil.

What can I cook tonight that is vegetarian?

Veg Hakka Noodles, Veg Stir-Fry & Noodles, Penne Arrabbiata, Mac & Cheese, Veg Burrito Bowl and Margherita Pizza are all vegetarian and all take 30 minutes or less.

How do I decide what to cook tonight?

Set three limits — time, diet and budget — then pick the first dish that fits all three. Or spin the wheel once with the Dinner Dilemma preset and cook whatever it lands on.
